## Key Findings

### CIC network properties
- CICs follow a power-law-like distribution: the vast majority are smaller than the mean (4.0E-4), and a small number are much larger.
- The CIC network is directed and asymmetric: C_ij ≠ C_ji in general. The network is also small-world-like with hub genes (APC, TP53, MLL3 are the three largest hubs).
- Hub genes are all tumor suppressors, consistent with an Internet-like architecture in which signaling hubs propagate information broadly.

### Inferred probable orders and validity
- Sum of all probable-order probabilities across 1,118 samples = 1,034.4 (ratio 1,034.4/1,118 ≈ 0.925), indicating the inference identified high-confidence probable orders in ~93% of samples.
- For the well-studied colorectal pair APC → KRAS, the inferred order probability was 0.95 — consistent with prior experimental and computational studies.
- For breast cancer samples with TP53 and BRCA1, the order TP53 → BRCA1 was inferred with probability > 0.99 (BRCA1 somatic mutations are known to occur after wild-type BRCA1 loss triggered by TP53 mutation).

### Tumor suppressors dominate initiation
- An overwhelming majority (>77.5%) of the probability-weighted probable orders are initiated by a tumor-suppressor gene, not an oncogene.
- Top initiators overall: TP53 (Times = 484.7, Rate = 0.844) and APC (Times = 117.5, Rate = 0.675), far exceeding any oncogene.
- Top oncogene initiators: PIK3CA (31.97, rate 0.381) and KRAS (24.09, rate 0.223) — an order of magnitude below TP53.
- Of 368 cancer genes in the test set, only 92 were tumor suppressors, yet they accounted for the dominant share of initiation events, ruling out a frequency artifact.

### Cancer-type-specific initiators (Table 3)
- Ovary: TP53 initiates 91.5% of probable orders (overwhelmingly TSG-dominated).
- Large intestine (LI): APC initiates 57.5%, TP53 29.7% — both TSGs; KRAS (oncogene) initiates 6.8%.
- HLT (haematopoietic/lymphoid): TP53 14.9%, NPM1 10.4%, MLL2 9.9% — more distributed, multiple TSGs; BCL2 convergence point (see below).
- Prostate: TP53 30.4%, APC 9.9%, MLL3 4.5% — TSG-dominated; oncogene initiators ~40% of orders (relatively elevated).
- Breast: TP53 57.6%, PIK3CA 11.8%, MLL3 4.7%.
- CNS: CIC 13.6%, PIK3CA 10.1%, TP53 10.0% — more heterogeneous.
- UAT (upper aerodigestive): TP53 73.4%.

### Apoptosis evasion as the earliest acquired hallmark
- In most cancers (ovary, LI, UAT, breast, prostate), the first mutated gene in the most probable orders functions in evasion of apoptosis.
- TP53, APC, KRAS, PIK3CA, NPM1, and CIC (the gene) have all been shown to produce deficient apoptosis when mutated.
- In HLT cancers, the three most frequent transitions all converge on BCL2 (TP53→BCL2, MLL2→BCL2, EZH2→BCL2), suggesting that acquiring anti-apoptotic BCL2 activity is a defining early event in liquid cancers.
- This aligns with Hanahan & Weinberg's hallmarks framework and advances it: evasion of apoptosis is not merely acquired but appears to be the *first* hallmark acquired in most cancer types.

### Additional transition structure
- APC → KRAS: 0.953 probability; APC → TP53: 0.656; TP53 → KRAS: 0.836.
- Three-gene colorectal order APC → KRAS → TP53: 0.330 (most probable of 6 possible orders in APC/KRAS/TP53 samples).
- In breast cancer: TP53 → BRCA2 (0.998) and TP53 → BRCA1 (>0.99) — both consistent with known biology.

## Limitations

- **No single-cell or longitudinal ground truth:** The inferred mutation orders are validated against prior literature for a handful of well-studied gene pairs (APC→KRAS, TP53→BRCA1) but lack systematic experimental ground truth for the vast majority of the 1,118 samples. Convergence of the iterative algorithm is a mathematical criterion, not biological validation.
- **COSMIC v61 is cross-sectional and sparse:** Co-occurrence frequencies aggregate across tumor types and stages; temporal ordering is inferred statistically, not from longitudinal sampling of the same tumors. The database reflects somatic mutations detected at a single time point.
- **Up to 8 mutant genes per sample:** Samples with more than 8 mutant cancer genes were excluded, meaning hypermutated tumors are underrepresented. The inference complexity (m! for m mutant genes) scales badly; even m = 8 requires 40,320 order evaluations per sample.
- **Coding point mutations only:** COSMIC v61 records coding point mutations; CNVs, structural variants, epigenetic alterations, and non-coding mutations are invisible to the CIC framework. The "order" inferred is therefore partial.
- **No clonal architecture information:** The method treats each mutant gene as equally present (clonal) in the tumor. Subclonal mutations and intra-tumor heterogeneity are not modeled; a mutation detected in a minority subclone could appear as a "late" event not because it occurred late in tumor initiation but because it is subclonal.
- **Parameter-free by design but assumption-laden:** The maximum-entropy initialization and the iterative convergence criterion (absolute difference in f_ij below a threshold) are choices that affect the inferred distributions, even if no explicit biological parameters are set.
