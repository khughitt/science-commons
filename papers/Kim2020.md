## Key Findings

### Data-derived findings (D)

**Pan-cancer prevalence.**
- **(D)** 460 of 3,212 tumor samples (14.3%) carried circular amplicons detectable by AA/AC.
- **(D)** ecDNA found in 25 of 29 cancer types analyzed; highest prevalence in aggressive histological subtypes: glioblastoma, sarcoma, and esophageal carcinoma.
- **(D)** ecDNA is nearly absent in matched blood/non-neoplastic tissue from the same patients — confirming somatic, tumor-specific origin.

**Oncogene enrichment.**
- **(D)** 38% of the 24 most recurrently amplified oncogenes were predominantly harbored on circular (ecDNA) amplicons rather than linear chromosomal segments.
- **(D)** Per-oncogene rates span 11% (PAX8) to 62% (CDK4) circular plurality; CDK4 is the most ecDNA-enriched.
- **(D)** 21.8% of 1,804 amplified oncogenes had circular plurality overall; this rises to 53.5% for highly amplified oncogenes (CN > 8). [UNVERIFIED: precise per-gene table from supplementary not accessible; headline figures from PMC main text confirmed]

**Gene expression and chromatin accessibility.**
- **(D)** Oncogenes on circular amplicons showed 1.2× higher expression than their linearly amplified counterparts (p < 0.0007).
- **(D)** Circular amplicons exhibited 1.2× more accessible chromatin than linear amplicons (p < 1×10⁻¹⁶).
- **(D)** Fivefold increased transcript fusion frequency in circular vs. non-circular amplicons (p < 1×10⁻¹⁴).

**Clinical outcomes.**
- **(D)** Patients with circular amplicons had significantly worse five-year survival vs. patients with non-circular or no focal amplifications.
- **(D)** Cox proportional-hazards model: 48% increased hazard ratio for circular amplicon patients (p < 0.001), controlling for cancer type and stage. [UNVERIFIED: exact covariate list in Cox model not retrieved from supplementary]
- **(D)** Circular amplicon patients had significantly elevated lymph-node spread at diagnosis (p < 1×10⁻⁵) and higher cellular proliferation scores (p < 1×10⁻¹⁵).

### Author interpretations (L)

- **(L)** "ecDNA amplification may enable tumors to reach high copy number of growth-promoting genes, while maintaining intratumoral genetic heterogeneity through its non-chromosomal mechanism of inheritance." The survival signal is presented as causal consequence of the ecDNA biology, not merely correlation with high CN.
- **(L)** The enhanced expression per copy number on ecDNA vs. linear amplicons is attributed in part to epigenetic mechanisms — enhanced chromatin accessibility and potential enhancer hijacking — though the paper does not formally dissect these mechanisms.
- **(L)** The uneven (non-Mendelian) segregation of ecDNA at mitosis is cited as the mechanistic basis for intra-tumor ecDNA CN variance; the survival data is taken to imply that this heterogeneity confers adaptive advantage to tumors.
- **(L)** ecDNA is framed as "a common event in cancer" and an "oncogenic driver" rather than a passenger; however, the study design is cross-sectional WGS and does not directly demonstrate clonal dynamics or selection over time.
- **(L)** The paper implies the AA/AC pipeline is a reliable field standard; sensitivity/PPV values are from cell lines and neuroblastoma, which may not fully represent detection limits in TCGA FFPE samples.

## Limitations

- **Cross-sectional design:** no longitudinal tracking; cannot demonstrate ecDNA dynamics or selective sweeps.
- **TCGA/PCAWG samples are largely bulk WGS at modest depth (~30–60×):** ecDNA at low CN or small size may be missed; prevalence estimate (14.3%) is likely a lower bound.
- **FFPE artifacts:** a substantial fraction of TCGA samples are FFPE; fragmentation artifacts could mimic or obscure circular amplicon signals.
- **AA/AC validation primarily in cell lines and neuroblastoma:** detection performance in TCGA tumor diversity is extrapolated, not directly measured.
- **Clinical outcome model:** 48% hazard ratio not stratified by ecDNA oncogene identity; confounding by cancer type or stage not fully excluded (cancer-type control is noted but stratification by driver gene is not detailed).
- **Mechanistic claims about expression and chromatin are correlative:** higher expression and accessibility on ecDNA are observed but not causally dissected (no perturbation experiments).
