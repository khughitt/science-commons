## Key Findings

- The paper reports confirmatory evidence that intermittent BRAF/MEK scheduling can be detrimental relative to continuous dosing.
- It argues that the negative result is unlikely to be explained only by the long half-life of trametinib in S1320, because a different BRAF/MEK schedule also failed to support the holiday hypothesis.

## Limitations

- This is still a fixed intermittent schedule, not a fully patient-specific adaptive rule.
- The trial does not directly measure sensitive/resistant competition as a prospective stratifier.
