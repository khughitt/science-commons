## Key Findings

- The trial randomized 206 patients, 105 to continuous dosing and 101 to intermittent dosing.
- Continuous dosing improved post-randomization progression-free survival compared with intermittent dosing, 9.0 versus 5.5 months.

## Limitations

- The trial tested fixed intermittent dosing, not patient-specific adaptive therapy.
- The design does not rule out a different biomarker-driven melanoma adaptive protocol.
