## Key Findings

- Seventy-nine patients were recruited, 39 to intermittent dosing and 40 to continuous dosing.
- Intermittent dosing was inferior across efficacy measures: median PFS 8.5 versus 10.7 months, median OS 18.1 months versus not reached, and ORR 57% versus 77%.
- Baseline BRAF V600E ctDNA was prognostic for worse OS, but conversion to undetectable ctDNA during treatment did not significantly predict better OS.

## Limitations

- Intermittent dosing was schedule-fixed rather than model-adaptive.
- ctDNA was analyzed as a prognostic/predictive marker, not as the control variable for adaptive switching.
