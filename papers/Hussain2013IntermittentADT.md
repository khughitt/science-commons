## Key Findings

- Median survival was 5.8 years with continuous therapy and 5.1 years with intermittent therapy.
- The trial did not establish noninferiority of intermittent therapy.
- Later adaptive-therapy modeling critiques this design because long induction can deplete the sensitive competitor population before cycling starts.

## Limitations

- This is an intermittent-therapy analog, not a direct test of adaptive therapy.
- It used fixed induction and broad PSA thresholds rather than patient-specific eco-evolutionary state estimation.
