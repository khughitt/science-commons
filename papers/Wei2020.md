## Limitations

- **MSS incompatibility (current implementation):** Validated only in MSI cells; completely fails (33% random accuracy) in MSS cells even at 184 division differences. The proposed improvements remain theoretical.
- **Cell-line only validation:** No primary tumor tissue profiled; human data limited to postmortem brain. Generalizability to primary cancer samples unknown.
- **Methylation arm is cell-type ID, not lineage clock:** Clarifies a common misread in reviews (including q057 framing in Mao2025 ref [65]) — the methylation component adds cell-type annotation, not temporal lineage resolution.
- **Low per-cell microsatellite yield:** ~1,217 loci/cell (HCT116), ~388 shared between pairs. At 10^-3 mutations/locus/division, ~0.39 new mutations expected per pair per division — marginal signal even in MSI cells.
- **Only two authors:** Small team; method has had limited independent replication as of 2020.
- **No primary human tumor samples:** The cancer claim is for MSI cell lines, not primary patient tumors; tissue heterogeneity and FFPE degradation are unaddressed.
