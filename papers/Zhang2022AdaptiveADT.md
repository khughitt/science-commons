## Key Findings

- Sixteen evaluable patients were enrolled.
- All evaluable patients had greater than 90% PSA decline by the end of induction, with induction limited to no more than 12 weeks.
- Testosterone recovery was rapid in many patients after treatment cessation.
- Some patients remained off treatment for more than 12 months without PSA or imaging progression.

## Limitations

- The study is single-arm and small.
- Long-term comparative efficacy against continuous combined ADT was not tested.
- The authors note that future models need ctDNA, AR alterations, histology, and spatial heterogeneity to improve burden-state interpretation.
