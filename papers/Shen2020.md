## Key Findings

### Operational definitions of plasticity

The paper uses a dual working definition: (1) **phenotype switching** —
discrete, bistable transitions between two or more stable states (modelled
by ODE bistability, e.g., EMT E/M switch); and (2) **continuous phenotypic
change** — a spectrum of phenotypes modelled by phenotype-structured PDEs.
The authors treat these as complementary rather than competing, and frame
the choice between them as a modelling decision that should match the
biology of the specific plasticity type (e.g., hysteretic EMT calls for an
ODE bistable; spatially heterogeneous drug-tolerance calls for a PDE).

### EMT and hybrid states

- EMT is reversible (MET occurs upon environmental change); tumour cells
  frequently occupy a **hybrid E/M state** with partial epithelial and
  mesenchymal properties.
- The TGFβ–ZEB1/miR-200 double-negative feedback loop produces hysteresis;
  mathematical and experimental work (Celià-Terrassa et al. 2018) showed
  the hybrid semistable state is a stable asymptotic branch in a bifurcation
  diagram — not merely a transient.
- EMT confers chemoresistance (e.g., resistance to third-generation
  EGFR-TKIs in NSCLC); TGFβ implicated in BRAF V600E melanoma and breast
  cancer EMT-related resistance.
- Collective invasion via hybrid partial-EMT is linked to worse clinical
  outcomes; breast cancer cells hijack stromal fibroblasts as leader cells.

### Drug-tolerant persisters (DTPs)

- Non-SCLC cells undergo a reversible phenotypic change to a **slow-cycling
  drug-tolerant persister state** during initial drug onslaught; similar
  DTP behaviour documented in glioblastoma and melanoma.
- BRAF V600E melanoma in vivo: drug removal led DTPs to switch to a
  proliferative state and sensitisation to vismodegib.
- A single-cell BRAF V600E melanoma study defined four DTP states
  (starvation-like SMC, neural crest stem cell-like NCSC, invasive,
  pigmented) with bifurcation at the SMC state mediated by PPARα (fatty acid
  oxidation) toward NCSC or pigmented fates.
- Whether DTPs are pre-existing or arise stochastically remains contested;
  Shaffer et al. 2017 showed rare melanoma cells transiently pre-express
  tolerance genes (e.g., AXL) before drug exposure, favouring a stochastic
  origin.
- **Adaptive mutability** is a secondary mechanism in DTPs: error-prone DNA
  polymerases (Polk, Rev1) are induced; genome-wide 5mC hypomethylation
  de-represses transposable elements, increasing mutagenesis rate.

### Transdifferentiation and lineage switching

- EGFR-driven NSCLC can convert to SCLC phenotype upon EGFR inhibition;
  rare EGFR-driven NSCLC transdifferentiates into neuroendocrine carcinoma
  histology. Transdifferentiated SCLC tumours retain the original EGFR
  mutation — confirming non-de-novo origin.
- Prostate cancer: neuroendocrine transdifferentiation in an
  androgen-receptor-independent manner (castration-resistant subtype).
- BCC: inhibition of Hedgehog pathway induces transdifferentiation toward
  mixed isthmus and IFE cell features.
- AML3: differentiation blockade at the promyelocytic stage due to chimeric
  PML-RARα; ATRA can partially reverse this (PML-RARα specific; not
  generalisable to other AML subtypes).
- The review frames these examples as compatible with "Lamarckian plasticity
  and Darwinian selection" acting together on a small subpopulation
  diverging prior to therapy.

### Microenvironmental and systemic drivers

- CAFs generate a drug-tolerant microenvironment "safe haven": BRAF
  inhibition activates MAPK/ERK in wild-type melanoma fibroblasts → integrin
  β1-FAK-Src → drug tolerance; CAF-secreted HGF binds c-MET on breast cancer
  cells → PI3K/AKT → HER2 inhibitor attenuation.
- TAMs co-evolve with tumour cells; M1→M2 polarisation is mediated by
  GPCR-sensing of microenvironmental acidosis in melanoma; M2 TAMs release
  immunosuppressive factors and IL6/TNF/CCL5/CCL18.
- Neuronal/systemic level: stress-elevated corticosterone induces
  TSC22D3 in dendritic cells, blocking type I IFN and abrogating IFNγ T-cell
  activation — stress-glucocorticoid surge can subvert therapy-induced
  anti-cancer immunosurveillance. Brain-derived BDNF secreted by
  differentiated glioma cells activates NTRK2 on GSCs (AKT survival).

### Plasticity vs genetic clonal evolution

The paper explicitly frames plasticity as **mechanistically distinct from
and orthogonal to** clonal genetic evolution. Key framing moves:
- Plasticity operates without new mutations; it is epigenetically encoded
  and enzymatically reversible — therefore better modelled as continuous
  (PDE/diffusion) than as discrete Darwinian steps.
- "Drug resistance may be induced by epigenetic enzymes, and any enzymatic
  activity, even with switching behaviour, is continuous" — this is the
  paper's clearest statement of the incommensurability claim (relevant to
  h002).
- The Waddington landscape can be "flattened" by insufficient immune control
  of differentiation (TOFT) or by oncogenic mutation (SMT) — both produce
  plastic populations, but via different routes.
- The review explicitly cites the phenotype-precedes-mutation framing of
  Pisco & Huang (2015) in the context of non-Darwinian dynamics in drug
  resistance (relevant to h004).
- Transdifferentiation examples (prostate → neuroendocrine; NSCLC → SCLC)
  are framed as Lamarckian-plasticity + Darwinian-selection acting jointly on
  a small pre-diverging subpopulation — a mixed model rather than pure
  non-genetic adaptation.

### Mathematical modelling frameworks

Figure 2 catalogues six modelling archetypes: (A) ODE compartmental
(sensitive/resistant); (B) ABM with drug-tolerant states; (C–F)
phenotype-structured PDEs incorporating selection, plasticity/diffusion,
and spatial structure. The paper advocates simple PDE models amenable to
optimal control for therapeutic scheduling; large ENT/Boolean network
systems are noted as biologically richer but analytically intractable.

## Limitations

- Focused on 2017–2020; many foundational references (Waddington, EMT
  classics) are cited but not re-examined — the review is a snapshot of a
  rapidly moving field and is now 5 years old.
- No primary data; all quantitative claims are inherited from cited primary
  papers, which vary in experimental rigour.
- The mathematical modelling section is advocacy for simple PDE/ODE
  approaches over richer stochastic models — the case for tractability over
  realism is asserted rather than benchmarked.
- The atavistic theory and TOFT sections (Section 4) are framed as
  "non-standard viewpoints" and treated with less critical scrutiny than the
  primary biology sections.
- DTPs are treated as a unified category, but the review itself notes that
  the pre-existing vs. stochastic-origin question is unresolved — a
  distinction that matters substantially for interpreting the
  non-genomic-adaptive-fraction claim.
