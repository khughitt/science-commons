## Key Findings

- The interim cohort included 11 adaptive-therapy patients suitable for comparison with 16 contemporaneous standard-of-care patients.
- At the interim readout, 1 of 11 adaptive-therapy patients had radiographic progression compared with 14 of 16 contemporaneous controls.
- Adaptive-therapy patients received 47% of the cumulative abiraterone exposure expected under continuous standard dosing.
- Patients were monitored with labs and PSA every 4 weeks and CT/bone scan imaging every 12 weeks.

## Limitations

- Interim sample size was small.
- The comparison was not randomized.
- PSA was treated as a tumor-burden proxy, but resistant fraction and lesion-level dynamics were not directly observed.
