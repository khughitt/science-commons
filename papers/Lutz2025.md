## One-Sentence Summary

Multi-modal single-cell and spatial analyses reveal that MM breakout lesions
harbor a tumor-microenvironment distinct from bone marrow, featuring "immune
islands" of clonally expanded, exhausted CD39+/PD-1+ CD8 T cells that co-evolve
spatially with divergent TP53- and HOXD3-mutated tumor subclones, identifying
these lesions as bidirectional evolution hotspots with implications for
immunotherapy failure in advanced disease.

> **Scope warning for consumers.** The single-cell-resolution *spatial* results
> (Key Findings 4 and 5) come from **one lesion in one patient (P02)**. Cohort-level
> replication is coarser and partial — see Methods and Limitations. Do not cite the
> spatial coevolution result as a cohort finding.

## Key Findings

1. **Breakout lesions harbor an ecosystem distinct from rBM and intramedullary
   lesions.** Multi-parametric flow cytometry across 54 matched samples from 16
   NDMM patients (23-plex identity panel: 7,609,355 cells across 54 ecosystems)
   showed that breakout lesions have mean plasma cell infiltration of 89.8% (range
   54.7–98.2%), a strongly enriched CD56bright/CD16− NK cell fraction, augmented
   Tregs, enriched CD39+/PD-1+ CD8 T cells (almost exclusively in BoL),
   mesenchymal stromal cells (MSCs), and endothelial cells. In contrast,
   hematopoietic stem/progenitor populations were severely depleted. Plasma cells
   from BoL upregulated CD16 and CD56 and downregulated CD19/CD27 compared to rBM,
   suggesting an adapted immunophenotype. Intramedullary lesions showed no such
   differences from rBM — but this negative rests on **n=5 ImL patients, unpaired**
   against the BoL patients (no patient contributed both lesion types), so it is
   absence of evidence at low power, not evidence of absence.

2. **CITE-seq reveals BoL-specific cell states and sustained tumor-immune
   interactions.** In five patients with paired BoL and rBM, CITE-seq of enriched
   cell fractions (13,057 plasma cells; 39,552 TME cells) identified: (a) a BoL-
   specific CD56bright NK subset with high exhaustion-gene expression, intermediate
   cytotoxicity, and reduced CXCR4, phenocopying NK exhaustion in solid tumors;
   (b) M2-like tumor-associated macrophages (TREM2/FOLR2) enriched in BoL;
   (c) enriched cDC1s; (d) stromal cells retaining LEPR but with reduced
   hematopoiesis-supporting factor production (CXCL12, KITL, IL7) and increased
   ECM output (COL3A1, SPARC); and (e) STAB2-negative endothelial cells with
   angiogenic signatures (ANXA1, STC1, IGFBP5) suggesting active neovascularization.
   Arterial ECs in BoL produced elevated CXCL12, likely mediating immune cell
   recruitment. The authors explicitly note **considerable interpatient heterogeneity**
   in these gene programs, "likely driven by patient-specific tumor–TME interactions."

3. **Breakout lesions contain clonally expanded, likely tumor-specific exhausted
   CD8 T cells.** CITE-seq with TCR-seq identified CD39+/PD-1+ CD8 T cells in
   BoL as predominantly expanded clones overexpressing CTLA4, LAG3, TIM3 (HAVCR2),
   TIGIT, and TOX. These cells highly expressed a neoantigen-reactive TIL gene
   signature from solid cancers and did not match pathogen-associated TCRs,
   suggesting myeloma specificity — though this is **negative evidence only**; no
   pMHC-multimer or antigen-reactivity assay was performed, and the authors say
   "likely" / "putative." **Scope:** the site-enrichment result (expanded clones
   not detectable in paired rBM) is attached by the paper to the **two patients
   (P01, P02) of the five CITE-seq patients** that showed particularly strong
   CD39+/PD-1+ CD8 enrichment — it is not established as a cohort-wide property.
   TCR diversity (Chao1) was reduced in BoL versus rBM stamps, but this is a
   **trend, not significant (P=0.06)**.

4. **Spatial analysis reveals microregional heterogeneity within a single BoL — n=1.**
   Dissection of one large BoL (patient P02) into 16 spatially defined regions
   (~63,671 TME cells by identity panel; ~46,442 T cells by T cell panel; 16,462
   CITE-seq TME cells, **region 12 failed so correlations use 15 of 16 regions**)
   showed that CD39+/PD-1+ CD8 T cells and cDC1s co-localize in the same
   microregions (R=0.69–0.72, P=0.002–0.004), while BoL-specific CD56bright NK
   cells anti-correlate with CD39+/PD-1+ CD8 T cells (R=−0.55, P=0.027). Two
   dominant spatial communities emerged: an antigen-experienced T cell cluster
   (with Tregs, cDC1s) and a plasma cell-dominated cluster devoid of
   antigen-experienced T cells. In the six additional spatially flow-profiled
   patients, this architecture was reproduced in the patient with **high**
   CD39+/PD-1+ CD8 levels (P11) but **not** in the patient with **low** levels
   (P05), who showed less spatial heterogeneity — so the architecture looks like a
   property of lesions with strong antigen-experienced T cell expansion, not of
   breakout lesions in general.

5. **Tumor subclonal evolution and T cell clonal diversification are spatially
   coupled — n=1 at single-region resolution.** WGS of six spatial regions plus
   paired rBM in patient P02 revealed gain(1q21) and KRAS^G12A as shared BoL
   founder events (present at minor subclonal level in rBM). Within the BoL two
   divergent branches evolved: Branch 1 (HOXD3^E333D, dominant in **R1**, minor
   subclonal in R2/R5) and Branch 2 (TP53^T284fs frameshift, dominant in distal
   regions R10/R14/R16), consistent with ongoing subclonal diversification under
   positive selection. TCR clone distributions mapped to the same spatial partition:
   TCR clone #3 was enriched in Branch 1 regions; clones #2 and #14 in Branch 2
   regions. Extended bulk WGS + TCR-seq across **8** additional patients (1–6
   regions each) **supports an association** — the paper's word — between the number
   of BoL-enriched TCR clones and the degree of spatial subclonal heterogeneity;
   BoL-enriched clones were found in only **6 of 8**. The authors report their own
   counterexample: differentially enriched TCRs also occurred between regions with
   *no* detectable mutational difference (R14 vs R16), leading them to conclude that
   "a combination of genomic **and microenvironmental** factors may underlie TCR
   heterogeneity." The coupling is correlational, not causal.

6. **Immune islands define the spatial architecture of breakout lesions.** IHC
   (CD8, CD68, MUM1) of 21 BoL and paired rBM identified focal immune infiltrates
   ("immune islands") surrounded by plasma cell-dominated areas in 9/21 BoL. No
   comparable pattern was found in rBM stamps. Multiplexed immunofluorescence
   (patient P02, Lunaphore COMET seqIF) resolved 14 cell types and 7 cellular
   neighborhoods: immune island centers (CN-1) contained CD4/CD8 T cells with
   CD39/LAG3/TIM3 co-expression and Ki67+ active proliferation, cDC1s in close
   proximity to CD39+ CD8 T cells (supporting antigen presentation),
   pericytes/endothelial cells, and blood vessels. M2-like macrophages (CD206+) and
   CD56bright GZMB+ NK cells could infiltrate plasma cell-dominated areas.
   Multiplexed imaging of 9 additional BoL confirmed immune islands across patients
   with interpatient variability in size and composition; the BoL-specific
   CD56bright NK phenotype (CD7+/CD56bright/GZMB+/CD16−) was detected in 6/9. Note
   **9/21 is a section-level detection rate, not a lesion-level prevalence**: in
   patient P01 the island was visible on the IHC specimen but not on the
   immunofluorescence specimen from the *same lesion*, so detection depends on which
   section was cut and 9/21 may under-count.

## Methods

- **Design.** Observational, **cross-sectional** (single-timepoint) multi-modal study of
  newly diagnosed multiple myeloma (NDMM). The comparison unit is a lesion type versus its
  paired random bone marrow (rBM). Not prospective, not interventional, no longitudinal
  sampling. Ethics: Heidelberg University Medical Faculty review board, **S278-13**;
  informed consent per the Declaration of Helsinki.
- **Cohort.** **54 matched samples from 16 NDMM patients**: **5 with an intramedullary
  lesion (ImL)** and **11 with a breakout lesion (BoL)** — *each patient contributed only
  one lesion type, so ImL vs BoL is a between-patient comparison, never within-patient*.
  Sample types: peripheral blood n=14, rBM aspirates n=8, rBM trephine biopsies ("stamps")
  n=16, ImL n=5, BoL n=11. A further **21 patients** (19 from the group's prior study)
  contributed trephine biopsies and/or WGS for validation.
- **Sampling route.** Osteolytic lesions identified by whole-body CT; focal lesions sampled
  under CT guidance; **surgical resections performed only in patients with a clinical
  indication for spinal stabilization** — so resected lesions are selected for size and
  symptoms. Processing: Ficoll for PB/aspirates; enzymatic digestion for BM stamps and
  resections; CD138+ immunomagnetic enrichment of MM cells.
- **Flow cytometry (n per panel).** 23-plex **identity** panel — 7,609,355 cells, 24 cell
  types, 54 "ecosystems" (all 54 samples). 23-plex **T cell** panel — 1,352,284 T cells,
  16 subsets. 23-plex **myeloid** panel — 703,084 cells, 11 subsets, **36 matched samples
  from 10 patients**.
- **CITE-seq + single-cell TCR-seq.** FACS-enriched plasma, myeloid, NK, T, stromal and
  endothelial cells from **5 patients** with paired BoL + rBM stamp → **13,057 plasma cells
  and 39,552 TME cells**. 10x 5' v2 with feature barcoding.
- **Deep spatial dissection — n = 1 patient.** One **particularly large BoL from patient
  P02** was cut into **16 microregions**: flow ecotyping (63,671 TME cells; 46,442 T cells)
  and CITE-seq + TCR-seq (16,462 TME cells; **region 12 failed, so single-cell spatial
  correlations use 15 of 16 regions**). *Every single-cell-resolution spatial result in this
  paper is n = 1.*
- **WGS.** Tumor cells FACS-sorted (CD38-high / HLA-DR− / CD3− / CD45+); Illumina NovaSeq at
  **85x tumor / 43x germline**; ACEseq (CNA), samtools mpileup (SNV), Platypus (indel),
  manual IGV refinement. **Spatial WGS phylogeny: 6 microregions (R1, R2, R5, R10, R14, R16)
  of the P02 BoL + paired rBM — n = 1 patient.** The main text does not state how many
  patients in the main cohort had WGS. [UNVERIFIED — do not cite a WGS patient count.]
- **Extended (cohort-level) spatial replication — coarser than P02.** Spatially resolved
  **flow cytometry** in **6 additional patients**; spatially resolved **bulk WGS + bulk
  TCR-seq** (Adaptive immunoSEQ, CDR3β) in **8 additional patients, 1–6 regions each**. An
  "enriched" TCR clone = ≥1% of the repertoire **and** fold-change ≥10 vs the rBM stamp or
  another region.
- **Immunohistochemistry.** CD8, CD68, MUM1 on **21 BoL and paired rBM stamps**; QuPath
  analysis with expert-pathologist review.
- **Highly multiplexed immunofluorescence.** Lunaphore COMET seqIF platform; **the paper does
  not state a plex count** (the "14" in this study is the number of *cell types identified*,
  not the panel size). Run on the **P02 BoL plus 9 additional BoL** (10 patients). The panel
  **differed between the two runs** — PAX5 was replaced by CD20 and CD7 added for the
  validation lesions.
- **Statistics.** Two-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum (unpaired) / signed-rank (paired);
  Benjamini-Hochberg correction; Pearson correlation or linear mixed-effects models; Mantel
  test for correlation-matrix similarity; flow data batch-corrected before integration.
- **Data availability.** Raw WGS / CITE-seq / scTCR-seq: EGA **EGAS50000000304** (controlled
  access via the DKFZ Data Access Committee, capped at 36 months). Processed single-cell
  data: GEO **GSE284727**. Validation-set WGS: EGA **EGAS00001006090**. No code repository is
  stated.

## Limitations

**Stated by the authors:**

- **No link from BoL immune parameters to clinical outcome.** The authors call this "an
  important next step" but did not do it, citing difficulty obtaining samples, limited
  follow-up under newly introduced processing procedures, and confounding by intra-lesion
  subclonal heterogeneity. **Every therapeutic implication in the paper (checkpoint blockade,
  bispecifics, CAR-T) is inferred from biology, not demonstrated against response data.**
- The functional role of the lesion-infiltrating NK and macrophage populations "remains to be
  clarified."
- **The genomic↔TCR coupling is not purely genomic**: differentially enriched TCR clones also
  occurred between regions with no detectable mutational difference (R14 vs R16), so "a
  combination of genomic and microenvironmental factors may underlie TCR heterogeneity."
- **Substantial interpatient heterogeneity** in stromal/endothelial gene programs and in
  immune-island size, composition and morphology — key features are conserved "in most"
  lesions, not all.
- Immune islands only **partly** resemble tertiary lymphoid structures; they are devoid of B
  cells (an author-hedged analogy, not an established TLS).

**Fair inferences from the reported design (not stated by the authors):**

- **The deep spatial findings are n = 1.** The microregional co-localization/anti-correlation
  architecture and the entire spatial tumor-subclone / T-cell-clone coevolution phylogeny
  (Branch 1 HOXD3 vs Branch 2 TP53) come from **one lesion in one patient (P02)**, with
  CITE-seq covering 15 of its 16 regions. Replication is coarser and partial: of the 6
  additional flow-profiled patients the architecture reproduced in the high-CD39/PD-1 patient
  (P11) but not the low one (P05); of the 8 bulk-WGS/TCR patients, BoL-enriched clones
  appeared in **6 of 8**, some with only one region sampled. **n=1 spatial results must not be
  cited as cohort-level.**
- **Small and unbalanced groups.** CITE-seq n=5; ImL n=5 vs BoL n=11, with no patient
  contributing both lesion types.
- **Selection toward large, symptomatic lesions.** Resected lesions came from patients needing
  spinal stabilization, and the dissected lesion was explicitly "a particularly large breakout
  lesion." Generalization to small or incidental BoL is untested.
- **True extramedullary disease (EMD) was never sampled.** The cohort is paramedullary
  (breakout) disease only; the immunotherapy-failure argument for EMD is an extrapolation.
- **Newly diagnosed, treatment-naive patients only.** Whether these features persist or
  amplify at relapse — where EMD and immunotherapy matter most — is untested.
- **Cross-sectional.** Evolutionary and "coevolutionary" claims are reconstructed from a
  single static timepoint; there is no temporal sampling.
- **Tumor specificity of the exhausted T cells is not proven** — the evidence is negative (no
  match to pathogen-TCR databases) plus a transcriptional signature. No pMHC-multimer or
  antigen-reactivity assay was performed.
- **No functional assays.** Exhaustion and NK/macrophage states are defined by protein and
  transcript phenotype only — no cytotoxicity, killing, or suppression assays.
- **The 9/21 immune-island rate is a section-level detection rate**, not a lesion-level
  prevalence (see Key Finding 6), and may under-count.
- **Data are under controlled access** (EGA, Data Access Committee approval, 36-month
  window), which limits independent reanalysis.
