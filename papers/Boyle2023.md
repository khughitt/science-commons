## Key Findings

1. **Focal vs. whole-arm gain(1q) are clinically distinct subtypes.** Of 302 patients with gain(1q), 219 (73%) have whole-arm gains (regions G2-G9) with adverse prognosis (PFS HR 1.3, p=0.04; OS HR 1.6, p=0.004) and 83 (27%) have focal gains associated with neutral outcome. Whole-arm gains are strongly linked to t(4;14), del(13q), del(16q), and shorter telomeres; focal gains are enriched for templated-insertion events (corr=0.34, BF=6e14) and hyperdiploidy.

2. **Fine mapping identifies 9 gain, 7 deletion, 2 templated-insertion (TI), and 3 chromothripsis (CT) regions on chr1.** Key gain-region drivers in A-compartment: MCL1 (G3), BGLAP (G4), SLAMF1/SLAMF7 (G5), POU2F1/CREG1 (G6), ABL2 (G7), BTG2 (G8), NLRP3 (G9). Key deletion-region tumor suppressors in B-compartment: CDKN2C/FAF1 (D3, bi-allelic loss in 62%), BCL10/FUBP1 (D5, bi-allelic 50%), RPL5/EVI5 (D6), TENT5C (D7, bi-allelic 26%).

3. **Whole-arm gain(1q) drives substantially broader transcriptional rewiring than focal gain.** Whole-arm gains: 2409 DEGs (101 transcription factors, including upregulation of BACH2, NR5A1, MYBL1, GLMP, E2F2, downregulation of PAX5, SMAD1, TBX21); focal gains: only 20 DEGs. GSEA of whole-arm gains highlights metabolic processes, biosynthetic processes, apoptotic resistance, and MAPK signaling.

4. **TENT5C super-enhancer hijacking drives oncogene upregulation at receptor sites.** TI1 (1p13.1-1p11.2) rearranges the TENT5C super-enhancer to receptor loci, upregulating genes there; NOTCH2 super-enhancer also activated in this region. TI2 (1q32.1-1q32.2) contains super-enhancers linked to BTG2 and MDM4.

5. **Hypomethylation at 1q21.3-1q23.1 provides an additional CNV-independent mechanism for gene overexpression.** 378 of 383 differentially methylated probes on chr1 are hypomethylated; 62 genes in gain(1q) patients are both hypomethylated and overexpressed, falling predominantly outside GISTIC2-defined gain boundaries. DDR2 and NTRK1 (oncogenes) identified as hypomethylated-overexpressed targets. EVI5, SLAMF7, and PDZK1 also hypomethylated.

6. **CRISPR dependency analysis identifies gain(1q)- and del(1p)-specific therapeutic vulnerabilities.** Genome-wide essentiality screen across 19 MM cell lines identifies 14 chr1-dependent genes preferentially essential in gain(1q) cells (including ZNF281, ZBTB37, PIGN, CD1D, HAX1, UCHL5, POU2F1, LAMTOR2, CCDC190). Three genes show increased dependency in del(1p) cells (ID3, GLMN, TRNP1) constituting candidate synthetic lethality targets. PSMD4 is highlighted as a gain(1q)-associated proteasome dependency with existing inhibitor evidence.

7. **Mutational landscape:** NRAS (17%) most frequently mutated on chr1; bi-allelic TSG inactivation common: CDKN2C (62%), FUBP1 (50%), TENT5C (26%). NRAS negatively correlates with CN cluster 3 (del(1p)/gain(1q)/del(13q)/del(16q)).
