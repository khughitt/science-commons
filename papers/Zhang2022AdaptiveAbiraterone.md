## Key Findings

- The study compared 17 adaptive-therapy mCRPC patients with 16 contemporaneous standard-of-care patients who met trial eligibility but chose continuous dosing.
- Median time to progression was 33.5 months in the adaptive group versus 14.3 months in the standard-of-care cohort.
- Median overall survival was 58.5 months versus 31.3 months.
- Adaptive patients received no abiraterone during 46% of time on trial.
- Updated model fits suggested stronger sensitive-on-resistant competition than assumed before the trial.

## Limitations

- The design was not randomized.
- Enrollment required a strong initial PSA response, creating responder-selection bias.
- The clinical observable was scalar PSA; resistant fraction, spatial heterogeneity, and lesion-level escape were inferred rather than directly measured.
