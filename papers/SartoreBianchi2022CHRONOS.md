## Key Findings

- Of 52 screened patients, 16 had RAS/BRAF/EGFR resistance alterations in ctDNA and were excluded.
- Twenty-seven patients received panitumumab rechallenge.
- The objective response rate was 30%, and disease control was 63%.

## Limitations

- Single-arm phase II design.
- Tests molecular rechallenge selection, not continuous adaptive dose cycling.
- Larger randomized trials are needed to compare ctDNA-guided rechallenge with standard late-line therapy.
