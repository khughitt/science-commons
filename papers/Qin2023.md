## Key Findings

### Data-derived findings (D)

- **(D1) Two distinct stem cell poles.** scRNA-seq and MC jointly identify revCSC (CLU^+^, slow-cycling, high stemness entropy) and proCSC (LRIG1^+^/EPHB2^+^, hyper-proliferative) as the primary stem cell attractors in the colonic epithelial landscape. Both are distinct from conventional LGR5^+^ CSCs. revCSC are transcriptionally similar to 'foetal' or 'revival' stem cells of the small intestine; proCSC are enriched in human and mouse CRC data.

- **(D2) Fibroblasts drive revCSC via TGF-β1 and YAP.** Fibroblasts polarise WT and *shApc* organoids to CLU^+^ revCSC through paracrine TGF-β1 → YAP signalling (not canonical β-catenin). YAP inhibition (CA3) partially reverses fibroblast-induced revCSC across genotypes. Fibroblast-induced revCSC show elevated pSOX9^+^, pRB [S807/S811]^+^ MAPK, and pMKK3/6 [S189/S207]^+^/pMAP-KAPK2 [T334]^+^/pP90RSK [T359]^+^/pSMAD2/3 [S465/S467]^+^, indicating a signalling signature distinct from WNT3A-alone revCSC.

- **(D3) APC-loss + KRAS^G12D^ collaboratively block revCSC access.** Fibroblasts can only induce revCSC in WT and *shApc* epithelia; adding KRAS^G12D^ abrogates this response. The combination (AK, AKP) hyper-activates cell-intrinsic β-catenin, MAPK, and PI3K while simultaneously downregulating ligand receptor expression, decoupling cells from stromal cues. K (KRAS^G12D^ alone) and KP organoids treated with R-Spondin-1 phenocopy AK/AKP, directly entering S-phase and the proCSC fate.

- **(D4) Continuous differentiation landscape governed by competing signalling flux.** TOBis MC of 180 organoid conditions (WENR permutation) reveals a continuous revCSC–equilibrium–proCSC trajectory regulated by multivariate combinations of oncogenes and stromal ligands, described by Boolean logic models: WNT3A and EGF together maintain revCSC–equilibrium balance in WT; KRAS^G12D^ rewires this logic so that even canonical stromal signals (R-Spondin-1) drive proCSC entry.

- **(D5) Oncogenic mutations reduce stromal-epithelial communication.** CellChat analysis shows WT and A epithelia have dominant incoming signalling from fibroblasts (4,138 and 4,014 interactions, respectively); AK and AKP epithelia show a sharp collapse (1,759 and 2,608 interactions), with loss of NRG1, EREG, IGF, and TGF-β fibroblast-to-epithelium interactions attributable to receptor downregulation in mutant cells. ProCSC are the "least receptive" cluster to cell-extrinsic cues.

- **(D6) Signalling pathway map of revCSC/proCSC competition.** Cue-signal-response array (432 MC organoid profiles) confirms: YAP is the central regulator of revCSC; PI3K and MAPK are central to proCSC identity. TGF-β1 + WNT3A reliably polarise WT/A epithelia to revCSC; EREG drives proCSC. PI3Ki treatment of AK organoids combined with TGF-β1 can partially rescue revCSC access, demonstrating residual plasticity in CRC organoids. SMAD inhibition does not alter TGF-β1-mediated revCSC regulation.

- **(D7) Waddington landscape reconstructed from data.** Integrating PHATE with Valley-Ridge score (signalling entropy + RNA velocity) produces a quantitative landscape: WT cells cluster at high-entropy stem peaks with TA valleys descending into secretory/absorptive fates; fibroblast co-culture erodes the TA valley (cells access revCSC slope); APC-loss + KRAS^G12D^ collapses the landscape so most cells occupy the proCSC peak with minimal differentiation potential.

### Author interpretations (L)

- **(L1) Cancer as chronic de-differentiation via oncogene-driven plasticity lock.** Authors frame CRC oncogenesis as a unidirectional shift toward de-differentiation, not through acquisition of an entirely new cancer-specific state, but by trapping cells in a pre-existing hyper-proliferative (proCSC) attractor while severing the stromal inputs that normally allow plasticity towards revCSC. "Cancer is a chronic, unidirectional shift in de-differentiation."

- **(L2) revCSC as candidate drug-tolerant persisters (DTP).** Authors propose that revCSC, being a slow-cycling, YAP-dependent state accessible in CRC organoids, may represent CRC DTP cells. CAF-induced revCSC could be targeted by combining YAP inhibitors (block revCSC access) with standard chemotherapy (kill proCSC). [This is an authorial speculation; the connection to in vivo DTP has not been experimentally validated in this preprint.]

- **(L3) Fibroblasts as master regulators of revival stem cells.** The authors generalise across intestinal contexts (citing small intestinal revival stem cell literature) to claim fibroblasts are a universal regulator of revival stem cell identity — here mechanistically grounded through TGF-β1/YAP signalling rather than canonical WNT. [Generalisation to human CRC CAFs and in vivo tumours remains untested in this work.]

- **(L4) proCSC are not a cancer-specific state.** Authors explicitly state proCSC are also present in WT organoids at low levels and highly enriched in WT + WENR culture — arguing proCSC represent an extreme of the normal differentiation spectrum occupied chronically by oncogenic mutations, not a neo-plastic novelty. [This framing has important implications for therapeutic targeting specificity.]

## Limitations

### Author-stated

1. **Healthy fibroblasts, not CAFs.** The fibroblasts used are healthy colonic fibroblasts (Cornell gift); cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs) likely communicate differently with CRC epithelial genotypes. Specifically noted that "CAFs will communicate differently with epithelial cells, particularly in CRC" — the signalling models built here may not transfer directly to the tumour stromal context.
2. **Macrophages do not regulate epithelial genotypes.** Macrophage transcriptomes in this system were only regulated by co-culture, not by epithelial genotype — limiting mechanistic insight into immune-epithelial coupling.
3. **WNT3A as WENR proxy.** The WENR ligand supplement is a canonical but incomplete proxy for the in vivo mesenchymal niche; WNT3A alone induces a distinct (more quiescent) revCSC than fibroblast-induced revCSC, confirming the diversity of stromal signals exceeds what WENR captures.
4. **revCSC-to-DTP connection is speculative.** Authors explicitly flag the therapeutic DTP model as a future direction; no drug-tolerance experiments are reported in this preprint.

### Additional limitations (not author-stated)

5. **Murine organoid system.** All organoids are mouse-derived; the degree to which revCSC/proCSC polarisation dynamics and the quantitative signalling thresholds (e.g., WNT3A vs. EGF concentration ratios) transfer to human CRC organoids or in vivo tumours is not established.
6. **Preprint status.** At the time of this PDF (2023-02-15), the manuscript had not undergone peer review. Key claims — particularly the Boolean logic models, the DTP hypothesis, and the Waddington landscape reconstruction — should be treated as provisional until published.
7. **Static co-culture snapshots.** The 48-hour culture windows provide a cross-sectional view; temporal dynamics of cell-state switching (rate constants, reversibility timescales) are inferred from RNA velocity and Boolean models rather than live tracking.
8. **No in vivo validation of revCSC/proCSC in CRC tumours.** The paper cross-references human CRC transcriptomic data (Figure S2A) to confirm proCSC gene signatures are present in vivo, but no primary human CRC spatial or single-cell data confirm the revCSC abundance/location in intact tumours. [UNVERIFIED — the supplemental Figure S2A is not available for direct inspection in this read session]
9. **Epistatic saturation.** The most extreme genotype (AKP — APC-loss + KRAS^G12D^ + p53^R172H^) is included in scRNA-seq but not all TOBis MC arrays; the additional contribution of p53-loss to proCSC trapping is incompletely characterised in the perturbation data.
