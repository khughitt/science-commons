## Key Findings

- The study enrolled 60 patients.
- Median PFS was 18.4 months, and median total treatment-break duration was 9.1 months.
- Fourteen patients remained in TKI cessation, 31 restarted because of ctDNA or CEA, and 15 restarted because of progressive disease.
- The TKI retreatment response rate was 96%.

## Limitations

- Nonrandomized, single-center design.
- The population is a highly selected post-TKI plus local-consolidation subset with no radiographically detectable disease.
- It does not directly test sensitive/resistant competition.
