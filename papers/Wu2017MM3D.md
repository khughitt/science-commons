## One-Sentence Summary

Integrated Hi-C, WGS, and RNA-seq of two aneuploid MM cell lines (RPMI-8226 and
U266) reveals that CNVs bias raw Hi-C interaction matrices, CNV breakpoints
preferentially co-localize with TAD boundaries, MM genomes display ~25% more
TADs of smaller median size than normal B cells, and ~20% of genomic regions
undergo A/B compartment switching accompanied by concordant changes in gene
expression and epigenetic marks.

## Key Findings

1. **CNV bias in Hi-C data and ICE correction.** Raw Hi-C interaction counts are
   positively correlated with copy number (Spearman r = 0.83 for RPMI-8226 WGS
   vs. Hi-C CNV estimates; F-test p < 2.2 × 10⁻¹⁶). ICE normalization reduces
   this bias more effectively than HiCNorm, producing a per-copy interaction map
   suitable for downstream analysis. The filtered Hi-C data independently reproduce
   CNV calls from WGS.

2. **Hi-C detects translocation events.** Inter-chromosomal interaction hotspots in
   Hi-C identify chimeric chromosomes arising from translocations. In RPMI-8226,
   4 of 56 WGS-detected inter-chromosomal translocations fall in the top-100 Hi-C
   interaction pairs (Fisher's exact p = 2.6 × 10⁻⁸; observed/expected = 90.9),
   including the t(16;22)(q23;q11) event involving WWOX and MAF. Combining Hi-C and
   WGS recovers additional translocation events implicating ADORA2B, FLII, AMBRA1,
   and PTPRJ in RPMI-8226, and TNIK, FBXW7, and TRIM23 in U266.

3. **CNV breakpoints co-localize with TAD boundaries.** In RPMI-8226, 7.5% of CNV
   breakpoints are TAD boundaries and 30.7% fall within 120 kb of a TAD boundary —
   significantly closer than location-randomized controls (Fisher's exact p = 0.038).
   Insulation score profiles at CNV breakpoints resemble those at TAD boundaries.
   The same co-localization is observed in normal B cells (GM12878), suggesting that
   CNV breakpoints either preferentially arise at TAD boundaries or that TAD-disrupting
   CNVs are selected against during cancer evolution.

4. **MM TAD fragmentation: more and smaller TADs.** ICE-normalized Hi-C at 40 kb
   resolution identifies 3457 TADs in RPMI-8226 and 3342 in U266, compared with 2756
   in GM12878 normal B cells (~25% increase). Median TAD size is 600–640 kb in MM
   vs. 800 kb in normal B cells. 1281 TADs are conserved across all three cell types;
   740 are shared between the two MM lines but absent from normal B cells.

5. **~20% A/B compartment switching with concordant expression changes.** At 500 kb
   resolution, ~8% of genomic regions switch A→B in one or both MM lines (associated
   with gene downregulation; t-test p < 2.2 × 10⁻¹⁶) and ~24% switch B→A (associated
   with upregulation). Genes in consistently switched regions are enriched for MAPK
   signaling, TNF signaling, cytokine–cytokine receptor interaction, N-glycan
   biosynthesis, and hematopoietic cell lineage pathways. A cytokine receptor gene
   cluster at 2q11.2-q12.1 (IL1R1, IL1R2, IL18R1, MAP4K4) switches A→B in MM with
   reduced H3K27ac, H3K4me1, and H3K4me3 marks and downregulated expression —
   without accompanying CNV, demonstrating regulation beyond dosage effects.
