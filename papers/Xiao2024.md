## Key Findings

### Data-derived findings (D)

- **(D) ClockDML loci are enriched in open chromatin across diverse cell types.** ClockDML (chronology set, 587,801 bp) are significantly enriched in ATAC peaks of pan-cancer (TCGA), bladder cancer, normal cells, placenta, and hematopoietic cells by Fisher's exact test (all Fisher odds ratios >1, p < 2.2e-16). This holds for the mitosis-associated ClockDML subset (Yang et al., 1,934 bp) and solo-WCGW loci.

- **(D) EpiTrace age increases with in vitro passage in primary MEFs.** In primary mouse embryonic fibroblast (pMEF) cells passaged serially in vitro, EpiTrace age stably increases with passage number (Supplementary Fig. 8). Within a single cell cycle, EpiTrace age transiently decreases in S/G2 phase and recovers in G1, consistent with a dilution-then-net-accumulation model. The passage-dependent net increase in EpiTrace age correlates well with DNAm-based age prediction (Supplementary Fig. 8). No exact per-passage or per-division numerical rate is reported in the main text; the relationship is shown graphically in supplementary figures.

- **(D) EpiTrace-predicted age matches ground-truth developmental order in zebrafish.** Mean EpiTrace age per developmental stage from fertilization to adult (log-transformed days post-fertilization) correlates at R = 0.97 across zebrafish scATAC data (GSE178969), using human ClockDML mapped by synteny.

- **(D) EpiTrace age predicts clonal expansion potential in human HSCs.** In CD34+ HSC in vitro expansion (GSE142745), initial clonal EpiTrace age is inversely correlated with subsequent log clonal expansion ratio: R = -0.66 (P = 2.3e-8) at day 14 and R = -0.57 (P = 0.00058) at day 20, with clonal identity tracked by mitochondrial SNVs.

- **(D) EpiTrace age is rejuvenated during iPSC reprogramming.** In primed hESC chemically reprogrammed toward 8-cell-like (8CL) state, both DNAm age and EpiTrace single-cell age decrease; correlation between mean EpiTrace age and mean DNAm age: R = 0.998 (P = 0.04); per-cell correlation: R = 0.526 (P = 1.9e-38).

- **(D) ChrAcc change precedes DNAm shift at ClockDML.** In human embryonic development scCOOL-seq and nanoNOME-seq datasets, ChrAcc shifts before ClockDML DNAm changes on the same molecule. Forced transcriptional activation (dCas9-p300) at ClockDML induces ChrAcc gain followed by DNA hypomethylation. Forced DNAm addition (ZNF-DNMT3A) does not change ChrAcc or EpiTrace age.

- **(D) Glioblastoma: EpiTrace resolves pre-malignant and malignant clone ordering.** In GBM sample CGY2349 (scATAC, GSE139136/163655/163656), EpiTrace identifies a pre-malignant cluster (cluster 7) with lower mitotic age than all malignant clones but higher age than normal clones. EpiTrace phylogeny places MDM4+ cells ancestral to MDM4+/EGFR+ and MDM4+/PDGFRA+ clones, with bifurcation interpreted as occurring early in malignant transformation. MDM4+/EGFR+ and MDM4+/PDGFRA+ cells have similar EpiTrace ages (consistent with neutral evolution post-bifurcation).

- **(D) EpiTrace outperforms pseudotime in hierarchical accuracy.** EpiTrace phylogenetic trees for the same developmental lineage show higher resolution and less variation than pseudotime (Supplementary Fig. 33). In one comparison, the EpiTrace phylogeny outperforms trees built from highly variable ATAC peaks.

### Author interpretations (L)

- **(L) ChrAcc changes on clock-like loci are phenotypically neutral.** The authors argue that because (a) ClockDML from PBMCs predict age in non-hematopoietic lineages, (b) EpiTrace age tracks accumulative mitosis count rather than developmental maturity (cortical neuron example), and (c) cross-species prediction works, the clock loci do not encode lineage-specific functional information. This neutrality is interpretive and not directly tested by cell-type-specific perturbation.

- **(L) EpiTrace provides higher temporal resolution than mtDNA mutation-based tracing.** The authors state that mutation-based lineage tracing (e.g., mtDNA SNPs) has "temporal resolution restrained by the low natural mutation rate" and that EpiTrace complements this by providing a continuous age signal. However, no head-to-head quantitative comparison of per-division clock rates is provided in the paper.

- **(L) EpiTrace clock is a universal property conserved across Metazoa.** Working in Drosophila (which lacks canonical DNMT) is used to argue that clock-like ChrAcc is an innate genomic property that predates DNA methylation. This is a strong evolutionary claim based on one invertebrate and is not mechanistically resolved.

- **(L) EpiTrace temporal resolution is sufficient for cancer clonal tracing.** The GBM application is presented as evidence that EpiTrace resolves clonal history in a human tumor. The interpretation of early branching evolution and neutral post-bifurcation evolution is inferential, as it rests on relative age rankings rather than absolute division counts, and on a single tumor sample.

- **(L) Younger HSC clones inherently dominate terminal differentiation output.** The CD34 experiment result (younger EpiTrace-age clones produce more terminal cells) is interpreted as intrinsic expansion advantage of younger progenitors. Alternative explanation — that younger clones happen to have more self-renewing phenotype for other reasons — is not excluded.

## Limitations

1. **No absolute per-division clock rate reported.** EpiTrace age is a relative rank within a population, not an absolute division count. Supplementary Fig. 8 shows passage-dependent increase in MEF cells but the rate in human cancer cells is not published.

2. **Cancer calibration is limited to one GBM sample.** The single-tumor GBM application precludes generalizing clock behavior in cancer. Normal PBMC ClockDML may not be optimal reference loci for cancer cells with large-scale chromatin remodeling.

3. **Sequencing depth dependency.** EpiTrace accuracy degrades with low sequencing depth (acknowledged in Discussion). This is a practical constraint for clinical-grade scATAC data.

4. **Highly imbalanced populations impair accuracy** (e.g., very rare cell types). Relevant for tracking rare plastic-state cells in a predominantly clonal tumor.

5. **ChrAcc dilution during S/G2 phase.** Within a single division cycle, EpiTrace age transiently decreases, meaning cells sampled at different cell cycle phases will have artifactually different ages. Cell cycle correction is not implemented in the standard workflow.

6. **Cross-lineage generalization not fully validated in cancer.** ClockDML from PBMCs are used across many tissues, but the extent to which large-scale cancer epigenome remodeling distorts the clock signal is untested.
