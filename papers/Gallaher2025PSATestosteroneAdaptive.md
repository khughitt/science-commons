## Key Findings

- At 55 months, 6 of 16 patients had progressed in the ongoing pilot cohort.
- Higher PSA at the end of induction, smaller PSA change during induction, and higher average PSA/testosterone ratio over the first cycle predicted progression groups.
- The model interpretation is that early progressors may carry a larger resistant fraction before therapy.

## Limitations

- Preprint, not peer reviewed at the time of this note.
- Small single-cohort analysis.
- Biomarker thresholds require prospective validation before use as switch rules.
