## Key Findings

**Genome-wide genetic overlap:**
- All 15 pairwise rg estimates are significantly positive after Bonferroni correction (P < 8.33 × 10⁻³); median rg = 0.435 (range 0.148–0.677).
- Strongest rg: CAD–HF (rg = 0.677); weakest: AF–PAD (rg = ~0.148).
- MiXeR Dice coefficients reveal extensive polygenic overlap even where rg is low — e.g., CAD–VTE: 290 shared causal variants (Dice > 0.2) despite rg = 0.182, with ~30% of shared variants showing discordant effects.
- CAD–HF is extreme: 93.3% of CAD-influencing variants are shared with HF.

**Local genetic architecture:**
- LAVA identifies 130 unique genomic regions with bivariate local-rg signals; 24 survive Bonferroni correction.
- Mixed-effect directions even in strongly correlated pairs: CAD–VTE has 14 positively and 5 negatively correlated local regions.

**Causal relationships (vertical pleiotropy):**
- Strong causal evidence for **CAD → HF** (GCP > 0.6, Bonferroni-corrected; OR = 1.33–2.72 in multivariate MR, strengthened in UKB-excluded data) and **VTE → Stroke** (confirmed by both LCV and MRlap).
- AF → HF shows trends toward partial causation but does not meet the strict GCP threshold.
- Most CVD pair genetic sharing is horizontal (shared upstream mechanisms), not vertical causation.

**Shared loci (horizontal pleiotropy):**
- 38 pleiotropic loci across CVDs (9 shared by >3 CVDs); 12 loci show strong multi-trait colocalization (PP > 0.70) with consistent directional effects.
- Most prominent pleiotropic locus: **LPA** (rs10455872, 6q25.3) — colocalized across all CVDs except VTE; associated with elevated Lp(a).
- Second-most: **7p21.1** (rs2107595 near TWIST1/HDAC9) — vascular diseases (CAD, PAD, Stroke); TWIST1 expression in vascular smooth muscle cells supported by artery aorta eQTL.
- Third: **19p13.2** (~11 kb upstream of LDLR) — all CVDs except AF; below PP threshold but strong meta-analysis significance.
- Shared loci colocalize with metabolic traits (blood pressure, lipids, glycemic traits, kidney/liver function), particularly via CASZ1 (1p36.22), FTO (16q12.2), and FGF5 (4q21.21).

**Gene/pathway enrichment:**
- Only 10.88% of lead SNPs, 12.77% of susceptibility genes (MAGMA), and 10.58% of tissue-specific genes (TWAS) reach genome-wide significance for >1 CVD.
- Shared gene set signals: **circulatory system development**, circulatory system process, and heart development enriched across multiple CVDs (but none in >2).
- GARFIELD: shared functional signals dominated by AF, CAD, and VTE (DNaseI HSS, chromatin accessibility, histone modifications); HF, PAD, and Stroke excluded from most shared regulatory categories.

**Two-group CVD structure:**
- **Group 1 (AF, CAD, VTE):** higher heritability, stronger genetic signals, more prominent shared biological mechanisms, richer co-enrichment in regulatory categories.
- **Group 2 (HF, PAD, Stroke):** weaker genetic signals, fewer overlapping mechanisms, more heterogeneous etiology.
- This two-group structure emerges consistently from pathway, tissue, functional category, and colocalization analyses, suggesting that clinical CVD classification conflates molecularly distinct entities.

## Limitations

- **European ancestry only:** Main analysis restricted to European-ancestry GWAS; East Asian replication provides partial validation but trans-ancestral generalizability unconfirmed.
- **Six CVDs only:** HF, PAD, and Stroke are underpowered relative to AF, CAD, and VTE, which biases the two-group structure toward the power-rich group and may obscure shared signals for the underpowered trio.
- **Common variants only:** GWAS captures only common SNPs; copy-number variants and rare coding mutations are absent, potentially missing a layer of shared architecture.
- **Proteome-wide MR inconclusive:** No circulating proteins with consistent causal associations across multiple CVDs were identified, partly due to platform differences (SomaScan vs Olink) and sample overlap.
- **Circularity in pathway interpretation:** Circulatory system development enrichment reflects the cardiovascular biology of the input diseases; this is expected and does not distinguish shared from disease-specific pathways in any novel way.
- **Dice coefficient underestimation:** When one trait has far fewer causal variants, the Dice coefficient underestimates true overlap (noted by authors for HF–PAD).
