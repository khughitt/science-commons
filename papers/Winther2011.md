## Key Findings

**Three distinct types of part-whole explanation in biology, each with a distinct
partitioning frame:**

| Explanatory type | Parts abstracted | Orientation | Explananda |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mechanistic | Low-level structure-parts + process-parts (genes, molecular activities) | Atomistic, bottom-up, causal | System behavior; mechanisms themselves |
| Structuralist | Hierarchical structure-parts + emergent processes; mathematical laws of form | Holistic, top-down, formal | Emergent form and development |
| Historical | Temporally changing organism-parts + species as parts of lineages | Temporal, phylogenetic | Long-term changes of parts and wholes |

**Parts are abstracted, not pre-given.** Partitioning frames are sets of
theoretical-experimental commitments that identify: (i) kinds of parts, (ii) part
relations (interaction, level, organization, precursor–product). Different frames
carve the same system differently and generally fail to map onto each other (they
cross-cut), though cells can serve as trans-frame reference parts.

**Case study — tetrapod limb under three frames:**
- *Mechanistic (geno-mechanistic)*: Shh/AER/ZPA gene-gradient axis system;
  positional information drives cellular differentiation into chondrocytes; 3-axis
  spatial grid established by sequential gene action.
- *Structuralist (self-organization)*: Newman-Hentschel reactor-diffusion model —
  eight coupled PDEs of the form ∂u/∂t = D∇²u + F(u) — where patterns emerge
  from cell-cell signaling and geometry rather than from a master gene program;
  local autoactivation–lateral inhibition (LALI) dynamics produce 1,2,N skeletal
  element patterns.
- *Historical*: Frame Shift Hypothesis (Wagner and Gauthier 1999) — avian embryo
  digit condensations C_II, C_III, C_IV give rise to adult digits D_I, D_II, D_III,
  accounting for conflict between paleontological and embryological data via a
  developmental frame shift.

**Explanatory complementarity:** No single frame is fundamental or subsumes
the others. The three projects focus on distinct levels, causal influences, and
temporal stages; together they provide a "floodlight" integrative vision.
Reification of any single project leads to ontological overclaiming.

**Inferential reliability as objectivity criterion:** A partitioning frame is
objectively valid when it generates reliable ampliative inferences — novel
predictions, projectible predicates, counterfactual support — not when it
corresponds to pre-existing natural joints.

## Limitations

- Focused on biological sciences; the claim of cross-disciplinary generality for
  part-whole explanation (Thesis 4) is asserted via examples rather than argued
  in depth.
- Formal mereology (P. Simons, Parts: A Study in Ontology, 1987) is acknowledged as adjacent
  but "requires further investigation"; the paper does not formalize the
  partitioning-frame concept mathematically.
- The "explanatory complementarity" defense is programmatic; no formal integration
  method for the three explanatory projects is provided.
- Winther does not address emergence formally: the claim that structuralist frames
  yield "genuinely emergent" properties rests on philosophical intuition rather than
  a precise criterion.
- History of the "systems tradition" (von Bertalanffy, Simon, Rosen) is mentioned
  in footnotes but not integrated into the main framework.
