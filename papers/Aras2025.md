## Key Findings

Premenopausal logs were characterized by menstrual-cycle-associated, digestive, mood, and integumentary symptoms.
Menopausal logs were characterized by vasomotor, pain, mood, and cognitive symptoms.
Perimenopausal users exhibited both menstrual-cycle-associated and vasomotor symptoms.
Fatigue, headache, anxiety, and brain fog were common across life stages.
Hot flashes poorly predicted symptoms other than night sweats.

## Limitations

The data are self-selected app logs with self-reported menopausal status and symptoms.
The study does not measure hormones, immune markers, infection, or cycle phase directly.
