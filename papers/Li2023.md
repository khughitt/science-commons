## Key Findings

**Cathepsin H → lung cancer (forward, univariable IVW):**
- Overall lung cancer: OR = 1.068 (95% CI 1.035–1.102), p = 3.4 × 10⁻⁵
- Adenocarcinoma: OR = 1.080 (95% CI 1.036–1.126), p = 2.6 × 10⁻⁴
- Squamous cell carcinoma: OR = 1.053 (95% CI 1.005–1.104), p = 0.032 (weaker, IVW only; not replicated by weighted median or MR-Egger)
- SCLC: OR = 1.054, p = 0.166 (not significant)
- Corroborated by weighted median and MR-Egger for the overall and adenocarcinoma results; no directional pleiotropy detected.

**Smoking-stratified subgroup:**
- Ever-smokers (univariable): OR = 1.095 (95% CI 1.055–1.138), p = 2.4 × 10⁻⁶
- Ever-smokers (multivariable, adjusted for 8 other cathepsins): OR = 1.092 (95% CI 1.049–1.137), p = 1.8 × 10⁻⁵
- Never-smokers: no significant association (small never-smoker arm, n = 9,859; underpowered).

**Multivariable MR (all 9 cathepsins jointly):**
- Cathepsin H retains independent association with overall lung cancer (OR = 1.070, p = 1.5 × 10⁻⁴) and adenocarcinoma (OR = 1.094, p = 8.9 × 10⁻⁵).
- Cathepsin G → adenocarcinoma signal (univariable IVW p = 0.041) disappears after adjustment — not independent.

**Reverse MR:**
- Squamous cell carcinoma → increased cathepsin B levels: IVW OR = 1.189 (95% CI 1.014–1.395), p = 0.033; weighted median p = 0.038. No pleiotropy. Cathepsin H shows no reverse causality.

**Mediation / colocalization:**
- No significant causal path smoking → cathepsin H (IVW OR = 0.919, p = 0.494) or cathepsin H → smoking.
- Colocalization at the CTSH locus (chromosome 15q25.1): posterior probability of shared causal variant with smoking = 0.004. Cathepsin H is an independent risk factor, not a smoking mediator.

## Limitations

- All participants European; results may not generalize to other ancestries.
- Never-smoker arm is underpowered (n = 9,859 out of 50,036 total); the null result in never-smokers is inconclusive.
- MR instruments from INTERVAL (n = 3,301) are modest in sample size for protein QTLs; F-statistics not reported explicitly.
- IV p-value threshold relaxed to 5 × 10⁻⁶ (vs. conventional 5 × 10⁻⁸) due to sample size constraints — instruments may be weaker than in larger pQTL studies.
- GWAS data are summary statistics only (two-sample design); individual-level mediation cannot be assessed.
- The squamous carcinoma → cathepsin B reverse MR result is nominally significant (p ≈ 0.03) and should be interpreted cautiously.
- Proposed mechanisms (angiogenesis, ECM degradation, ERK activation) are speculative; the paper cites prior cell/animal work but provides no direct mechanistic evidence.
